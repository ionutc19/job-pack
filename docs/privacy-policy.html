Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Job Coach ("the App") is a mobile application developed by Ionut Popa. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use the App.

1. Data Processing

When you use Job Coach, your input — including CV text, job descriptions, and LinkedIn profile sections — is sent to our backend server for processing. This data is transmitted securely over HTTPS and used solely to generate your requested analysis, profile improvements, or cover letter.

2. AI-Powered Results

Job Coach uses artificial intelligence models hosted on Microsoft Azure to generate results. Your input is forwarded to the AI model as part of the request. The AI processes your data in real time and does not retain it after generating a response.

3. Data Storage

Job Coach does not permanently store your CV text, job descriptions, or generated results on our servers. Input data is processed in memory and discarded immediately after the response is delivered.

The following data is stored:

  • On your device: Language preference and a randomly generated user ID (UUID). No personal information is stored locally.
  • On our server: Usage counters (request counts per module), subscription status, and entitlement data to enforce plan limits. No input text or generated content is stored.

4. File Uploads

When you upload a document (PDF, DOCX, or TXT), the file is transmitted securely to our server for text extraction. The file content is processed in memory only — it is not stored persistently, logged, or retained after processing. Only the extracted text is used for your current request and then discarded.

5. Subscriptions & Payments

If you subscribe to a paid plan (Premium or Pro), your subscription status and usage data are processed and stored to enforce plan limits. All payment processing is handled entirely by Google Play — we do not collect, store, or have access to your payment details, credit card numbers, or billing address.

6. Advertising

The free tier of Job Coach displays banner advertisements served by Google AdMob. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect device identifiers, advertising IDs, and usage data according to Google's Privacy Policy. Premium and Pro subscribers do not see advertisements.

7. Third-Party Services

Job Coach uses the following third-party services:

  • Microsoft Azure (OpenAI Service) — for AI-powered text analysis and generation
  • Google Play Billing — for subscription management and payment processing
  • Google AdMob — for serving advertisements on the free tier

Each service operates under its own privacy policy. We encourage you to review their respective policies.

8. Data Security

All data transmitted between the App and our servers is encrypted using TLS/HTTPS. We follow industry-standard security practices to protect your information during transmission and processing.

9. Children's Privacy

Job Coach is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

10. Your Rights

Since Job Coach does not permanently store your input data, there is generally no personal data to request deletion of. Your locally stored user ID can be reset by uninstalling and reinstalling the App. If you have questions about your data, please contact us.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

12. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please contact us:
